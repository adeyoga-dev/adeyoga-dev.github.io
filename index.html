    Hi Fellas 👋

    I'm a fullstack web developer with around 3 years of hands-on experience building and maintaining web applications. My core stack revolves around Laravel, PHP, JavaScript, and MySQL, where I focus on creating scalable, maintainable, and efficient systems — not just features that "work," but solutions that last.

    I started my journey as a generalist, exploring various technologies such as Python, Java, Docker, and basic Linux administration. This exposure helped me understand systems from different angles — from backend logic to deployment environments.

    Currently, I'm in the phase of leveling up from a generalist into a more specialized and mature developer. I care deeply about clean code, structured architecture, and writing systems that are easy to extend and debug.

    In my projects, I prioritize:

    • Clear and maintainable code structure
    • Practical problem-solving over overengineering
    • Performance and scalability considerations
    • Real-world usability

    I enjoy turning ideas into real products, whether it's a simple CRUD system or a more complex workflow-based application. For me, development is not just about coding — it's about building solutions that actually solve problems.
